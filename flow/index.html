Evolved hepatic PV-HA-HV 0D flow controller

An evolved controller informed by the earlier hepatic-circulation model. PV, HA, and HV values change together under physiology-anchored inputs; the controller is HABR-informed and illustrative, not a validated subject-specific clinical prediction. Default PV is 800 mL/min; use 600–900 mL/min for fasting sensitivity and about 1050 mL/min for a postprandial stress case.

Integrated scenario

The transition interpolates between an intact state and a right-hepatectomy remnant state. With HABR enabled, the remnant HA anchor is applied; with HABR disabled, HA is held at the intact baseline as a counterfactual. Both cases are scenario visualizations, not time-resolved pressure solves.
